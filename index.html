What is MatrixQR?

MatrixQR is a free & secure client-side QR code generator designed for modern digital privacy needs. Unlike traditional online QR tools that process data on remote servers, MatrixQR renders custom QR codes directly within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and local JavaScript execution. This architecture guarantees zero server logging, zero data transmission, and complete offline capability for generating WiFi passwords, phone contact links, and vCards.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MatrixQR completely free to use?

↓

Yes, MatrixQR is 100% free with no hidden fees, limits, or mandatory registration required to export high-resolution QR codes.

Does MatrixQR collect or store my WiFi password or personal contacts?

↓

No. All data processing occurs entirely within your browser using local JavaScript execution. Your WiFi credentials, phone numbers, and vCards never touch any external server.

How do I scan a WiFi QR code generated by MatrixQR?

↓

Simply open your iOS or Android camera app, point it at the generated QR code, and tap the notification banner to join the wireless network automatically without typing the password.
