What is MatrixQR?

Why Are Traditional Online WiFi QR Generators a Security Risk?

Most online QR generators send your inputs to remote backend servers. When generating a WiFi QR code, your network name (SSID) and WPA/WPA2 passwords are transferred over the internet, leaving traces in server logs and third-party tracking databases.

How Does Client-Side WiFi QR Code Generation Protect Your Password?

MatrixQR renders QR codes 100% locally in your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript execution. Zero network payload leaves your device during matrix generation, keeping sensitive credentials completely private.

Can You Generate a WiFi QR Code Completely Offline with MatrixQR?

Yes. Once the web application is loaded into your browser cache, you can safely turn off your internet connection or switch to airplane mode to generate WiFi, contact, and text QR codes with full functionality.

MatrixQR is a free & secure client-side QR code generator designed for modern digital privacy needs. Unlike traditional online QR tools that process data on remote servers, MatrixQR renders custom QR codes directly within your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and local JavaScript execution. This architecture guarantees zero server logging, zero data transmission, and complete offline capability for generating WiFi passwords, phone contact links, and vCards.
