How to Create a Secure WiFi QR Code

Sharing WiFi passwords is a hassle. Typing long strings of characters on a small phone screen is frustrating. Here is how to solve it using MatrixQR.

Why use a QR Code for WiFi?

WiFi QR codes use a standard string format (WIFI:S:MyNetwork;T:WPA;P:MyPassword;;) that smartphones can natively understand.

  • Security: No need to write passwords on a whiteboard.
  • Speed: Guests connect in seconds.
  • Aesthetics: Looks professional in cafes or offices.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Open MatrixQR.cc.
  2. Select the WiFi QR tab.
  3. Enter your Network Name (SSID) exactly as it appears.
  4. Select your encryption type (usually WPA/WPA2).
  5. Enter your password and click Generate.

Pro Tip

If you have a "Guest Network" on your router, generate a QR code for that instead of your main network. It keeps your IoT devices safe!
